## What “empirical assurance” means

Absolute outcome guarantees are not technically honest for agentic or visual work. This roadmap instead requires a falsifiable evidence ladder: strict contracts, observed RED/GREEN, actual diff enforcement, independent verification, immutable build identity, clean-checkout recovery, cross-harness replay, human editorial approval, blind comparison and measured cost. A stage remains `blocked`, `failed` or `unverified` whenever one of its mandatory proofs is absent.
